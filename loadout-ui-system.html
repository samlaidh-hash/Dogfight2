LOADOUT SYSTEM GUIDE

Overview

Configure your aircraft's weapons and equipment before each mission. Your loadout affects both your combat capability and aircraft performance.

How to Use

  • Click a hardpoint (circle on aircraft) to select it
  • Choose a store from the right panel to mount it
  • Compatible stores are highlighted; incompatible ones are grayed out
  • Use Symmetric Loading to auto-mirror left/right pylons
  • Click Quick Loadouts for pre-configured setups

Performance Impact

External stores add weight and drag, reducing your aircraft's performance:

  • Green bars (80-100%): Minimal impact
  • Yellow bars (60-79%): Moderate degradation
  • Red bars (<60%): Significant penalty

Balance firepower against maneuverability based on your mission!

Store Types

  • Air-to-Air Missiles: Lock-on guided weapons for dogfighting
  • Bombs: Unguided gravity weapons for ground targets
  • Rockets: Unguided projectiles, good vs soft targets
  • Anti-Radar: Homing missiles for SEAD missions
  • Fuel Tanks: Extended range, minimal combat penalty
