# SAM Evasion Tactics Guide - Dogfight 2

## Understanding the Threat

### Warning Indicators

1. **Yellow "SAM THREAT" Warning** (Bottom-Right)
   - You're within radar detection range
   - SAM is searching or has detected you
   - Not locked yet - you have time to react
   - Shows: SAM type, range, radar status

2. **Red "RADAR LOCK" Warning** (Bottom-Right)
   - SAM is tracking YOU specifically
   - Lock percentage shows how close to missile launch
   - 80%+ lock = IMMINENT LAUNCH (flashing)
   - Shows: SAM type, range, lock %

3. **Red "MISSILE INBOUND" Warning** (Top-Center)
   - One or more SAM missiles are actively pursuing you
   - Flashing red - maximum urgency
   - Shows: Number of incoming missiles
   - TIME TO EVADE!

### SAM Types and Their Threats

#### SA-2 Guideline (Medium Threat)
```
Range: 30km
Speed: Mach 2
Best At: Medium altitude (500-5000m)
Weakness: Low altitude, terrain masking
```
**Counter**: Fly below 300m, use terrain, break lock with hard turns

#### Patriot (High Threat)
```
Range: 70km
Speed: Mach 3.5
Best At: All altitudes
Weakness: Extreme low altitude (<100m)
```
**Counter**: Stay VERY low, use speed, expect salvos

#### S-300 (Extreme Threat)
```
Range: 150km
Speed: Mach 5
Best At: Everywhere
Weakness: You. (Good luck.)
```
**Counter**: Pray. Seriously though - extreme evasion, terrain masking, hope the reload catches you

#### ZSU-23-4 Shilka (Short-Range Threat)
```
Range: 2.5km
Type: Rapid-fire AAA
Best At: Low altitude (<500m)
Weakness: Range, altitude
```
**Counter**: Stay high (>2000m) or stay FAST and low

## Evasion Tactics

### Before You Enter the Threat Zone

1. **Plan Your Route**
   - Check minimap for SAM locations (red markers)
   - Identify terrain features for masking
   - Note altitude requirements for mission

2. **Choose Your Approach**
   - High altitude: Safe from AAA, vulnerable to SAMs
   - Low altitude: Terrain masking, but AAA threat
   - Medium altitude: Worst of both worlds - AVOID

3. **Speed is Life**
   - Enter fast, exit faster
   - Never slow down in the threat zone
   - Use dive to build speed if needed

### When You See Yellow Warning

1. **Situational Awareness**
   - Note SAM type and range
   - Check your altitude vs SAM envelope
   - Identify escape routes

2. **Defensive Positioning**
   - Get terrain between you and SAM if possible
   - Adjust altitude to SAM's weakness
   - Increase speed

3. **Don't Panic Yet**
   - Yellow warning = detected, not locked
   - You have time to maneuver
   - Complete attack run but be ready to break

### When You See Red Warning (RADAR LOCK)

1. **Immediate Actions**
   - Note lock percentage
   - Prepare for hard evasion
   - Monitor for missile launch

2. **Pre-Launch Evasion**
   - Break line of sight with terrain
   - Hard turn away from SAM
   - Reduce altitude rapidly
   - Change heading erratically

3. **Launch Indicators**
   - Lock >80% = flashing warning
   - Watch SAM site for smoke plume
   - "MISSILE INBOUND" warning

### When Missile is Inbound (RED ALERT!)

#### The 4-Step Survival Process:

**1. AWARENESS** (First 2 seconds)
- How many missiles?
- Your current speed and altitude?
- Terrain nearby?

**2. DEFENSIVE MANEUVERS** (Immediately)
- Hard turn (90° or more)
- Change altitude (dive or climb)
- Break toward terrain if available

**3. MAINTAIN ENERGY** (Critical!)
- Don't bleed too much speed
- Avoid stalling during evasion
- Use gravity to maintain speed in turns

**4. KEEP EVADING** (Until missile misses or explodes)
- Don't fly straight
- Continue erratic maneuvers
- Watch for missile to pass or explode

#### Specific Evasion Techniques:

**The Break Turn**
```
1. Wait until missile is close (~3-5 seconds from impact)
2. Hard turn 90° perpendicular to missile
3. Pull maximum G (but don't stall!)
4. Missile can't match your turn rate
5. It overshoots and misses
```

**Terrain Masking**
```
1. Dive toward terrain
2. Get below 200m if possible
3. Put hill/mountain between you and missile
4. Missile loses radar lock
5. Impacts terrain or self-destructs
```

**The Dive**
```
1. Nose down, gain speed
2. Become harder target (faster = harder to hit)
3. Can lead to terrain masking
4. Builds energy for escape
```

**The Last-Second Jink**
```
1. Wait until missile is very close (<2 seconds)
2. Sudden, violent roll and turn
3. Missile can't adjust in time
4. Only for skilled pilots!
```

## Mission-Specific Tactics

### Mission 11: "Into the SAM Envelope" (SA-2)
**Strategy**: Low and Fast
- Approach at 200m altitude
- Use terrain to mask from radar
- Pop up only for attack
- Dive back down immediately

### Mission 12: "Patriot Challenge" (Patriot)
**Strategy**: Speed is Key
- Stay below 100m
- Maximum speed at all times
- Single pass - don't linger
- Expect 2-missile salvo

### Mission 13: "The Shilka Gauntlet" (ZSU-23-4)
**Strategy**: High-Low-High
- Approach at 3000m (above Shilka range)
- Dive on Shilkas first
- Destroy them quickly
- Then attack ground targets safely

### Mission 14: "S-300 Fortress" (S-300)
**Strategy**: Extreme Evasion
- Stay below 50m if possible
- Use ALL terrain features
- One attack pass MAX
- Be ready to evade immediately
- This is HARD - don't be discouraged!

### Mission 15: "Mixed Threat Environment"
**Strategy**: Prioritize Threats
1. Kill Shilkas first (low altitude threat)
2. Then SA-2 (medium threat)
3. Deal with Me-109 (air threat)
4. Ground targets last (static)

## Advanced Tips

### Energy Management
- Speed = Life in SAM environment
- Always know your minimum safe speed
- Use terrain dive to regain energy
- Don't turn fight near SAMs

### Timing Your Attack
- Attack during SAM reload phase
- Watch for "RELOAD" indicator on SAM
- 30-40 seconds of vulnerability
- Plan multiple runs around reload times

### Altitude Bands
```
>2500m: SAM primary zone - DANGEROUS
500-2500m: Medium altitude - WORST
100-500m: Low altitude - Safer from SAMs
<100m: Very low - Risk vs reward
```

### Reading the Battlefield
- Radar beams show SAM attention
- Green beam = scanning (searching)
- Red beam = pointing at you (tracking)
- No beam = radar off (rare)

### Using Wingmen
- Order wingman to attack SAM
- While SAM tracks wingman, you attack
- Coordinate attacks from different angles
- Share the threat

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Flying Straight**
   - Never fly straight in SAM zone
   - Always be turning or changing altitude
   - Predictable = dead

2. **Wrong Altitude**
   - Medium altitude (500-2500m) is the kill zone
   - Commit to high or low
   - Don't linger in the middle

3. **Ignoring Warnings**
   - Yellow warning = prepare to evade
   - Red warning = evade NOW
   - Flashing red = MISSILE LAUNCH

4. **Slow Speed**
   - WW2 fighters are slow vs missiles
   - Every bit of speed helps
   - Never enter threat zone below cruise speed

5. **Tunnel Vision**
   - Watch multiple SAMs
   - Check minimap
   - Don't focus only on ground target

6. **Multiple Passes**
   - One pass is dangerous
   - Two passes is risky
   - Three passes is suicide
